EDWARD'S
JOURNEY

It is a time of struggle. Edward Houston, a 42-year-old warrior, fights daily battles against forces that would break lesser souls.

HOMELESS. No family support. No identification to access the systems designed to help. No shelters. No traditional employment. No bank accounts. No food assistance.

ONE MONTH CLEAN from the grip of addiction. Each day, a victory against alcoholism and narcotics that lurk in the shadows, always within reach.

LIVING WITH SCHIZOPHRENIA. The confusion makes completing tasks difficult. The constant worry about what comes next—the next hour, the next day—creates stress that compounds every challenge.

But Edward has not surrendered. Armed with donated, obsolete technology that crashes and freezes, he teaches himself the ways of CYBERSECURITY, WEB DEVELOPMENT, and BACKEND PROGRAMMING.

Every day, he searches for Wi-Fi. A cup of coffee at McDonald's becomes a gateway to knowledge, a portal to a different future.

He is building skills that will change his destiny. Learning code while others sleep. Studying security protocols on devices that barely function. Creating a path forward when all conventional roads are blocked.

This is not just survival. This is transformation.

One day at a time. One line of code at a time. One small act of support at a time.
